Geriatric assessment

GeriTrack

Every older outpatient's assessment and risks, in one view.

For
Geriatric clinics and the clinicians who assess older outpatients.
Before
Assessments on paper and in spreadsheets, with falls and muscle-loss risks spotted one patient at a time.
  • Register patients and record each assessment in one place.
  • Sarcopenia, sarcopenic obesity, poor muscle and fallers tracked across the whole clinic.
  • Risk flags for falls, mobility decline, grip-strength decline and bone protection.
  • A daily outpatient census, population graphs and one-click data export.

How a project runs

Four habits, in the order a project meets them.

They're why the products above are still in use.

Press and hold. Each habit lights up in the order a project meets it.
  1. 01

    Watch the work first

    Before we design anything, we sit with the clinician, carer or freelancer doing the job and note where the time actually goes.

  2. 02

    Ship something small

    A working slice in people's hands beats a long spec. We release early, watch it being used, then build the next piece.

  3. 03

    Say plainly what it does

    No “AI-powered solutions”. If it flags a patient's falls risk before the fall, that's what we call it, and that's what we test against.

  4. 04

    Stay for the boring part

    Most software fails after launch. We keep maintaining what we build, and you talk to the people who wrote it.

Why it matters

Most software fails after launch. We stay for the boring part: the updates, the fixes, and a person you can call.
