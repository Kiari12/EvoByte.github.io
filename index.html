How we work

Structure first. Decoration never.

We're a small team, so we keep the process small too. Four habits cover most of it.

  1. 01

    Watch the work first

    Before we design anything, we sit with the teacher, nurse or admin doing the job and note where the time actually goes.

  2. 02

    Ship something small

    A working slice in people's hands beats a long spec. We release early, watch it being used, then build the next piece.

  3. 03

    Say plainly what it does

    No "AI-powered solutions". If it marks 200 scripts in an afternoon, that's what we call it, and that's what we test against.

  4. 04

    Stay for the boring part

    Most software fails after launch. We keep maintaining what we build, and you talk to the people who wrote it.
